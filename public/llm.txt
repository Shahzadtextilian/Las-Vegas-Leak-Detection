## Diagnostic Equipment & Methodology
1. **Acoustic Frequency Filtration**: Amplifies the precise sonic signature of pressurized water escaping tiny fissures beneath concrete slabs, asphalt, and historic crawlspaces.
2. **Infrared Thermal Thermography**: Maps surface temperature gradients to reveal hot-water slab lines, freeze-burst wall leaks, and hidden moisture saturation.
3. **Helium / Hydrogen Tracer Gas**: Non-toxic, inert gas pressurization detected at parts-per-million levels through Eagle Valley soil, driveways, or frost-depth mains.
4. **Endoscopic Video Inspection**: High-definition fiber-optic cameras inserted into sewer, drain, and ventilation conduits.
