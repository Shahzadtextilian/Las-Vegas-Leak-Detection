## Core Non-Invasive Services
- [Water Leak Detection](https://www.leakdetectionnv.com/water-leak-detection/): High-sensitivity acoustic correlators and FLIR thermal cameras to trace pressurized water supply pipe failures behind finished drywall, ceilings, and under cabinets without exploratory drywall cuts.
- [Slab Leak Detection](https://www.leakdetectionnv.com/slab-leak-detection/): Pinpointing pressurized copper and PEX hot/cold domestic lines ruptured under concrete slab foundations. Eliminates unnecessary jackhammering; enables direct spot repairs or overhead pex repiping.
- [Pool & Spa Leak Detection](https://www.leakdetectionnv.com/pool-leak-detection/): Electronic listening sticks, structural dye tests, skimmer throat inspections, and hydrostatic pressure testing for swimming pools, spas, suction lines, and return runs in desert heat.
- [Gas Leak Detection](https://www.leakdetectionnv.com/gas-leak-detection/): Digital ppm sniffer devices, ultrasonic sensors, and sensitive manometer drop tests to locate dangerous natural gas and propane line leaks before health and fire hazards escalate.
- [Underground Leak Detection](https://www.leakdetectionnv.com/underground-leak-detection/): Ground-penetrating acoustic sensors and harmless tracer gas to locate fractured underground irrigation lines, main water service runs, and desert caliche soil crushed conduits.
- [Emergency Leak Repair](https://www.leakdetectionnv.com/emergency-leak-repair/): 24/7 rapid response dispatch with fully stocked vans capable of immediate shutoff isolation, bypass repiping, copper soldering, PEX manifold installation, and emergency valve replacements.

## Diagnostic Equipment & Methodology
1. **Acoustic Frequency Filtration**: Amplifies the precise sonic signature of pressurized water escaping tiny fissures without drilling.
2. **Infrared Thermal Thermography**: Maps surface temperature gradients to reveal hot-water slab lines, moisture pockets, and hidden wall saturation.
3. **Helium / Hydrogen Tracer Gas**: Non-toxic, inert gas pressurization detected at parts-per-million levels through soil, asphalt, or concrete.
4. **Endoscopic Video Inspection**: High-definition fiber-optic cameras inserted into sewer, drain, and ventilation conduits.
